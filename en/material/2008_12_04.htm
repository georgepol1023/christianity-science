Fossils – the Cambrian explosion

Holy Scripture, in the 1st chapter of Genesis, repeatedly makes a very serious statement: all kinds of plants and animals were created by God “after their kind”. That is, all the kinds of plants and animals as we know them today were created from the beginning, and the characteristics of a kind were never altered to such a degree that a new kind of plant or animal arose, as the theory of evolution claims.

But which of the two positions (creation or evolution) is confirmed by the finds that the science of palaeontology has brought to light?

Palaeontology is the branch of science that studies the evidence that tells us about living beings and about the environmental conditions on Earth during earlier geological periods, that is, millions of years ago. The basic source of information for palaeontology is fossils, that is, the remains or traces of animals or plants buried in rocks from an earlier geological period. Fossils form when an organism dies and is buried. Then, if the conditions are right, either the organism is covered by minerals and, as it decomposes, its “mould” remains, or the organic matter of the plant or animal is replaced molecule by molecule by minerals. In the process of forming a fossil, the harder parts of the organism (bones, shells and so on) are more likely to be preserved, while soft tissues are preserved less often, although there are very many cases where, because the conditions were right, soft tissues have been preserved too. There are also cases where whole animals have been preserved intact in materials such as ice and resin (for more details see the article “Fossils” in the right-hand column).

By studying fossils, palaeontology gathers information about the forms and way of life of the animals and plants that lived millions of years ago, and also about the landscape and climate of those times. So fossils rightly bear the name “nature's archive”.

Returning to the dilemma “evolution or creation?”, let us see how fossils can support one view or the other. If the theory of evolution is true, the various geological rock layers should be full of fossils showing the various intermediate, transitional forms of organisms as they evolved from one kind to another. So, starting from the beginning of life on Earth, the fossil record should first contain simple single-celled organisms, then slightly more complex many-celled organisms with simple organs, and the complexity should increase little by little until fully formed animals and plants appear.

But in reality nothing of the sort happens. The fossil record reveals a very strange event that happened 500 – 600 million years ago, which is called the Cambrian explosion. While before this period the fossils show only simple single-celled organisms on Earth, quite suddenly there is an abrupt appearance (an “explosion”) of complex organisms (at first marine ones). From the Cambrian period onwards, there are successive sudden appearances of all the known animal and plant types (phyla) that exist to this day. That is, in the fossil record, without any simpler ancestral forms, complete organisms suddenly appear, fully developed and with complex systems (for example, animals with a complex muscular system, a complex nervous system, complex skeletal joints, a system of blood circulation and clotting, and specialised organs for walking, swimming, catching food and so on). This problem for the theory of evolution was already known in Darwin's time (19th century), when palaeontology was still a young science. But from then until today, when palaeontology has developed spectacularly and has far more fossils at its disposal, the problem of the lack of intermediate, transitional forms in the fossils (the so-called “missing links”) remains unsolved.

Like the question of irreducible complexity, Darwin had also set the question of the lack of transitional forms as a test of whether his theory stood. He himself writes in “The Origin of Species”:

“To the question why we do not find rich fossiliferous deposits belonging to these assumed earliest periods prior to the Cambrian system, I can give no satisfactory answer... The case at present must remain inexplicable; and may be truly urged as a valid argument against the views here entertained”

Indeed, since apart from the sudden appearances that followed the Cambrian explosion no new animal types (phyla) have appeared since, this amounts to an act of Creation! From the single-celled organisms that existed before, complex organisms suddenly appear without any earlier transitional forms, and these basic animal and plant types (phyla) exist to this day!

Indeed, the attempts to give extinct species found as fossils the label of “missing link” either remain in the realm of hypothesis without any proof, or are cases of fraud, or the “missing link” turned out not to be so “missing” after all, since it was found alive in our own day without having undergone any evolution (so it was not a “link” either). A typical example is the coelacanth fish, which was thought to be the missing link between fish and amphibians, and which was caught several times during the 20th century, with exactly the same form that the fossils show it had millions of years ago (for more – not so – “missing links”, see the article “Living Fossils” in the right-hand column).

So if in 500 – 600 million years (that is, from the Cambrian explosion to today) no evolution has taken place, how, in the few million years of the transition from the Precambrian to the Cambrian period, was there time for such a rapid and dramatic evolutionary process to take place, and how could it ?

An attempt to explain the Cambrian explosion in evolutionary terms was made in the 1970s, when two researchers, S. J. Gould and N. Eldredge, put forward the theory of punctuated equilibrium. According to this theory, evolving species appear to remain static (in a state of “equilibrium”) for very long periods. But when large-scale natural disasters happen, these states of equilibrium are interrupted (“punctuated equilibria”) and organisms undergo mutations so dramatically that new species suddenly arise (note: this theory in effect disagrees with the evolutionary process as Darwin set it out, since he held that evolution happens through gradual, step-by-step changes). But such a theory cannot stand, because from the science of genetics we know that:

  • So-called “favourable” or at least non-destructive mutations are extremely rare, and for a new species to arise, a great number of “favourable” and non-destructive mutations must successfully accumulate.
  • These mutations must happen in the earliest stages of the organism's life (the embryonic stage) in order to cause large-scale changes and bring about the move to a new species. But mutations at this stage are as a rule destructive or even not viable.

So we see that the conclusions that follow from the findings of palaeontology vindicate the truly remarkable choice of the writer of Genesis to go against the ideas of his time about living organisms evolving from earlier monstrous creatures, and to declare that all living organisms were created “after their kind”.

Here we see once more that the Word of God is given by inspiration of God, because this statement is confirmed today by the knowledge that each kind has different genetic information (a different genetic code – DNA). Genesis also tells us that after the creation of man, God ceased from all His work of creation. Indeed, today we know that after the appearance of man, no new species has been observed to come into being (on the contrary, species are disappearing because of human intervention).

So this is knowledge that the writer could not have had, and which is the product of divine revelation.
