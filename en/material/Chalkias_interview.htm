Interview with the writer and publisher Christos Chalkias

Interview with the publisher and writer Christos Chalkias:

• Archaeology began 150 years ago with people who believed that the ancient texts contain truths and tried to confirm it.
• Two key discoveries that gave archaeology a push: the deciphering of cuneiform writing from Babylonian tablets and the deciphering of hieroglyphs from the Rosetta Stone.
• Archaeology began to develop, and at some point the question arose whether there would be finds confirming the biblical accounts.
• Biblical archaeology is now a chair at many universities, because of the reliability of Holy Scripture, which helps research and the linking of archaeological finds with the accounts it contains.
• Archaeology is a science and does not accept evidence that “forces” Holy Scripture to be proved true. It rests on real and fully verified evidence.
• Examples of archaeological finds that confirm Holy Scripture: the food ration of the captive king Jehoiachin, the bed of King Hazael of Damascus, the Merneptah Stele, which is the oldest mention of the people of Israel, an inscription that mentions Balaam as a real person, and the pool of Siloam, discovered only in 2007.
