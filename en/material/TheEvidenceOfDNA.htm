NOTABLE QUOTES

  • The geneticist Michael Denton says that a teaspoon full of DNA could contain all the information needed to build the proteins of every kind of organism that has ever lived on Earth, and there would still be room left to store all the information in all the books ever written. An astonishingly efficient way of storing information! It is the most efficient way of storing information in the universe.

- Lee Strobel -

  • Neo-Darwinism and the theories related to it will not be able to survive the discoveries of 21st-century biology
  • The existence of information, from our everyday experience, is proof of the existence of an intelligence that produced it. Nature can produce random signals and structures, or at best patterns. It cannot produce information! Information requires the existence of intelligence.
  • Many people try to explain the origin of the information [stored in DNA] by referring to the working of the laws of physics and chemistry and to the chemical properties of the individual parts that make up DNA. That is like trying to explain the arrangement of the letters of a sentence printed on paper by referring to the physical and chemical properties of the ink as it sits on the paper. Chemical laws do indeed explain why the ink stays on the paper. But they do not explain how the ink is arranged so as to form a sentence with meaning.
  • Many people believe that the view of intelligent design rests on the implication that God exists. This implication is indeed present in the view, but the view itself does not rest on it; rather, the evidence from newer scientific discoveries (molecular biology, the laws of physics, cosmology) leads inevitably to it

– Stephen C. Meyer, philosopher of science, Discovery Institute, Washington –
