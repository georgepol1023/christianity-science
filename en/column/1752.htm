Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

Man, the object of God’s love

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

November 2017 · The “Christianity & Science” team

“…Then I (wisdom) was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, … and my delights were with the sons of men.” (Proverbs 8:30–31)

The Word of God, which cannot lie, the Bible, reveals this great truth to us: that God loves man. Indeed, the Lord Jesus Christ assures us that “…God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life” (John 3:16).

Man today enjoys an unprecedented wealth of knowledge in every field of science. But arrogance, supposedly wanting to set man free and put him in God’s place, has presented and still presents a distorted explanation of the facts. Man is thrown out of the centre of God’s love into the chaos of insignificance, with all the fatal consequences we see around us.

The line of reasoning by which the world is served the mistaken impression that science has overturned the facts of the Bible is as follows:

Copernicus, with the heliocentric system, drove the Earth out of the centre of the world, putting it in its real place: an insignificant grain of sand in the universe, an ordinary planet among so many others that must have life.

Darwin, two centuries later, drove man out of the centre of creation, revealing his animal origin, and that his existence is simply the result of a random, blind process (evolution).

The modern view (which is the result of the evolutionary view of the world) that the mind and self-awareness are products solely of the biological working of the human brain gave man the final blow, revealing to him that he cannot even claim to be the centre of himself.

It was thought that the results of this reasoning would lead man to psychological liberation, progress and the strengthening of his personality, because they would lead him to “awareness of reality”. Yet we see that belief in the purely random and animal (and so insignificant in the universe) origin of man takes away life’s meaning and so makes the main goal the pursuit of personal advancement and physical pleasure.

But such a worldview is a failure and mistaken, not only because it is responsible for the confusion and misery that mark modern human societies, but also because all three steps of the reasoning above are refuted by the latest scientific findings:

The idea that the mind and self-awareness are products of the biological working of the brain, and that there is no soul, is refuted by scientific studies showing that consciousness and reason continue to exist in cases of damage to an extensive part of the brain, or even when it stops working completely.

The theory of evolution is overturned by the latest findings of molecular biology, medicine, palaeontology and other fields of science, which point to the intervention of a Creator and not to random evolution, as Darwin maintained. Indeed, a recent detailed biography of Darwin and study of his work seals this (1).

The planet Earth is not a negligible “grain of sand” in the universe, one of countless planets on which life happened to appear. On the contrary, it is the only planet that has the right combination of all the conditions necessary for life (the anthropic principle). If even one of these parameters (of which there are more than 330) had a slightly different value, life on Earth would be impossible. What is more, it is statistically impossible for all these conditions to exist together, each to the degree required, anywhere else in the universe.

The Earth is indeed a very small planet in the vast universe, but not at all a chance one. Put simply, science today recognises that the universe was made so that life could exist on Earth.
It is not the centre of the universe, nor of the solar system, but it is the centre of God’s interest. And He showed this interest not by placing the Earth at the centre of the universe, as various religious systems thought (in any case the universe has no centre), but by the practical expression of His love: He shaped the Earth into a welcoming environment in which to place His beloved creature, man (who really is the centre of God’s creation).

What is more, on this planet God, although He Himself transcends the whole universe, chose to walk as a man, in the person of Jesus Christ, to shed His blood on the cross for the sins of every person, and to give him eternal life through His resurrection.

1. A. N. Wilson, Charles Darwin: Victorian Mythmaker (October 2017)
