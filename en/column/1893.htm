Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

A letter from the past

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

June 2019 · The “Christianity & Science” team

Many times (since 2008) our dear readers, and the listeners to our broadcast on Radio Christianity (104.3 FM), have asked us about the relationship between science and the Word of God, the Bible. With his simple and penetrating words, our dear brother Leonidas Fengos took a position on this in the following article in the newspaper “Christianity” in April 1997:

Should science lead people away from God?

This question is very serious and to the point. Science studies, investigates and classifies all knowledge and the latest conclusions…

Everyone respects science, because it really has explained many phenomena that had been inexplicable since the beginning of human life. Natural phenomena such as clouds, rain, hail, snow and fog, lightning and thunderbolts, were always a problem for people, who in their ignorance gave explanations that had nothing to do with the truth. The appearance of worms in water, in fruit or in mud, and the causing of infectious diseases, were also dark mysteries for many centuries.

For these and many other things science has given correct explanations and has shed light on many dark places. Science investigates, but it often comes up against facts for which it cannot give experimental explanations. In these cases science puts forward hypotheses, which are called theories. Many times these theories have proved wrong and have been withdrawn by science itself.

Scientists must be uninfluenced and completely objective, so that they can establish real facts, whether or not they can explain the conditions and the mechanisms.

Science cannot turn against God and try to find evidence to lead people away from faith in God. Of course it is not science itself that does this, but some scientists who are possessed by unbelief and negativity towards the existence of God and His works.

This has happened ever since science was called on to explain to us how the world was created, the living things on the earth, and above all life. Science was loaded with such a great responsibility by those people who rejected God as Creator of the universe and Giver of life. When man rejects the only truth, every explanation he gives is false, and sooner or later it is disproved.

The theory of spontaneous generation, for example, seemed reasonable at first, when people saw worms come to life in drinking water, and in fruit and meat left exposed to the air. This theory was of course disproved when Pasteur showed by experiment that the worms did not arise by themselves but from the eggs laid by various insects. Science proposed and supported the theory of spontaneous generation, but science itself came along with experiments and demolished it, finally and irrevocably. The trouble is that some scientists are so fanatical against God that they do not hesitate to use any evidence they think helps their unbelief, announcing it with a thousand comments and trying to lead people away from God.

While everyone admits that modern man (Homo sapiens) is a few thousand years old, some insist on throwing out phrases of unbelief. “Man,” they say, “appeared millions of years ago”, although they know very well that even the hardest-line scientists have put man’s age at 30,000 years, and school textbooks say 20,000 years.

In this way they create confusion, especially among people who are not able to check the truth because they do not have the relevant education. And there have been people who, in the fanaticism of their unbelief towards God, have even tried deliberately to deceive the world and drag it away from God. One of these was the Englishman Dawson, who found a skeleton together with a skull in a village, Piltdown. He took them home, put them in various chemicals and treated them so that they would look extremely ancient. It was triumphantly announced that the missing link between ape and man had at last been found. Years later the whole fraud was exposed, but it was announced in the small print of the news. L. F.

There was a period in human history when anyone who believed the Word of God exactly was treated with ridicule, or at least with contemptuous condescension, by those who “knew”. But the scene has changed. Scientific achievements are coming to agree once again with the Bible, and to vindicate those who held to the absolute truth of the Word of God. “All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them. They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.”

(Proverbs 8:8–9).

The “Christianity & Science” team

Published in the team's column in the newspaper “Christianity”, June 2019.
