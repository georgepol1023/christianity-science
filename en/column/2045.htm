Does atheism have answers? (Part 5)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

March 2021 · The “Christianity & Science” team

How does atheism explain the existence of man? (2)

In our previous feature we saw that man cannot be the product of random biological processes of evolution, since the human body is a real engineering marvel, from the stage of development in the womb onwards.

“Thy hands have made me and fashioned me”

(Psalm 119:73)

From the moment an egg is fertilised, wonderful mechanisms are set in motion that will lead to the complete formation of a new human being and finally to its birth, about 9 months later. None of these processes happens by chance; everything is directed precisely by “instructions” recorded chemically in the DNA molecule, which the new organism inherits half from each parent. The formation of all the structural parts of the body, the making of all the necessary chemical substances, and the way all the organs and systems work are determined by the information written in this molecule. It is present, identical, in each of our roughly 40 trillion cells, and although it is about 2 metres long, it is “packed”, with the help of special mechanisms, so that it fits into the nucleus of a cell about 10 millionths of a metre across! Its capacity for storing information is such that just one teaspoon of DNA could hold more information than all the books ever written.

And which of the processes that take place during pregnancy could we mention without seeing the wisdom behind it? First, care has been taken that each reproductive cell contains half the genetic information, so that at fertilisation the normal amount of genetic material results in the new organism. About 30 hours after fertilisation, the original cell (the zygote) begins to divide, forming a mass of cells (the blastocyst) which by the 7th day will implant in the womb, while at the same time the hormone gonadotropin stops menstruation. From the 3rd to the 7th week, on the one hand the placenta develops, and on the other cell differentiation takes place: one of the most wonderful processes, in which genetically identical cells give rise to all the different kinds of specialised cells that make up the organism (muscle, nerve, bone and so on). As is well known, during pregnancy the baby grows inside the amniotic sac, which contains the amniotic fluid that protects it from shocks and helps keep a steady temperature. We could write much more about the formation of the nerve cells and the brain, the heart starting to beat, the growth of the skeleton and internal organs, the production of blood, and so on.

How sharply reality contrasts with the statements of spokesmen for atheism, such as Peter Atkins, who regards human existence as nothing more than “a bit of slime on a planet”, or Richard Dawkins, who says that we are “survival machines – robot vehicles blindly programmed to preserve the selfish molecules known as genes”.

Yet hundreds of years before all these wonderful processes by which every human being comes into existence were uncovered, David had already been informed by the Holy Spirit, and his heart overflowed with gratitude to God for His glory:

“For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother’s womb. I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.” (Psalm 139:13–14)
