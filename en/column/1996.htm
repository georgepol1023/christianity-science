Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

The fear of the pandemic and the triumph of Christ’s Resurrection

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

May 2020 · The “Christianity & Science” team

“…who hath abolished death, and hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel…” (2 Timothy 1:10)

“The coronavirus pandemic (Covid-19) will permanently change the world order. The world will no longer be the same after it has (we hope) been successfully overcome.” This is the view of a politician of international standing, which he set out recently in an article. And it is not only pestilences (pandemics). A host of worldwide problems are destabilising daily life and bringing fear and insecurity into people’s hearts.

Recently scientists protested strongly that climate change is coming much faster than they had calculated (!). Soon political and economic refugees will be a negligible number compared with the waves of desperate climate refugees. Rapidly increasing seismic activity, and unrest, conflicts and so on, make up a nightmarish picture. And this is only the beginning.

Coming back to the pandemic, we marvel at how quickly it all happened. In December 2019, in the city of Wuhan in central China, a cluster of patients with pneumonia appeared. On 9 January 2020 it was announced that they were caused by a new strain of coronavirus. The virus and its consequences spread like wildfire. Within days it was announced that the epidemic had become a pandemic. As these lines are written, two million six hundred thousand people have fallen ill and 180,000 have lost their lives. In this time each country reacted differently. Some saw the coming danger as an exaggeration, perhaps even deliberate deception. They announced measures very late and paid dearly. For example, Spain took (hesitant) measures 30 days after the first death on its soil, and Italy after 18 days. So far the two countries have counted thousands of victims. Other countries ventured for a while to follow the example of Great Britain, which at first did not want to take any measures. But it too, terrified, began to take measures late, paying the heavy price of delay. The same and worse happened, and is happening, in the USA…

Greece, a small country just coming out of a long and severe economic crisis, facing an enormous wave of migration, hostile provocations from a neighbouring country and a fragile National Health System, was in theory lost. But the world was astonished to see something amazing. By 19 April only (…) 117 deaths from the coronavirus had been announced, and 2,000 cases in total.

The reason? Greece reacted immediately and effectively. The reaction time was only four days. The measures were harsh but (according to the experts) necessary. Within a few days there was a complete ban on movement (except in special, certified situations). Suddenly we found ourselves in lockdown. Simply going out was forbidden, allowed only in special cases, and felt like an expedition. We stay at home, we work from home, our shopping is brought home. We worship God from home… The churches are empty. The internet is on fire. Fear roams freely… Yet for Greece, words of praise arrive from many countries and international organisations.

What happened? We know well, because it is the Word of God, that only the Lord can build and keep a house safe (Psalm 127:1–2). The church of Christ has a command from the Lord to pray fervently for those who govern (1 Timothy 2:2–3). The eyes of the whole world are on Greece. If all of us humble ourselves, call on the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ and put away sin from our homes, great blessing awaits our country. The saying of Adamantios Korais will be fulfilled: “The Gospel will save Greece.”

If we put our hope in any other person we will be bitterly disappointed. Instead of walking by faith in the risen Jesus Christ, we will be sunk by the waves to come.

The matter is a personal one, like every question of faith. Before us lies a unique and glorious event: the Rapture of the Church! Christ, who conquered death, is coming to receive His Church, to keep it from all that is going to happen during the seven years of the antichrist… (Revelation 3:10)

And to return to the thoughts we began with: events are now worldwide in scope, but the solutions proposed are local and run counter to globalisation. Indeed, the latest crisis has shown, if nothing else, a dramatic lack of charismatic leading figures.

After the Rapture, that one (the antichrist) will appear, who, using economic domination as his lever, will impose complete control over man through the mark.

The Church waits, and hastens to meet her Bridegroom, Jesus Christ!

Christ is risen!

The “Christianity & Science” team

Published in the team's column in the newspaper “Christianity”, May 2020.
