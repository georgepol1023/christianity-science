Implants with artificial intelligence against mental illness (Part 2)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

March 2018 · The “Christianity & Science” team

In our previous article we described the new development in electronic implants.[1] A new great leap is about to be made in this field, as American researchers are developing the first brain implants that will have artificial intelligence. Their aim is the “automatic” treatment of mental illness.

The whole venture is under the auspices of the American Pentagon, which has stated that it wants to use implants of this kind to treat soldiers and war veterans suffering from conditions such as depression, post-traumatic stress and obsessive-compulsive disorder.
We remind our readers that these innovative implants will have special sensors that record neural activity, and will “run” artificial intelligence algorithms that, when they judge that something is wrong, stimulate the brain with electrical pulses so as to change the person’s emotions and, as a result, his behaviour. In other words, these devices decide by themselves when to intervene in the working of the brain to bring it back to normal levels, using small-scale electric shocks.
It is obvious, however, that a technology that aims to intervene in a person’s emotions and behaviour could make him the puppet of whoever programs the device – or of the device itself, which is able to decide on its own, since it has artificial intelligence.

The Word of God, which cannot lie, prophesied two thousand years ago the coming of a worldwide dictatorship under the rule of a leader who will be completely opposed to everything Christian, and who is described as the antichrist, or the beast:
“And he causeth all, both small and great, rich and poor, free and bond, to receive a mark in their right hand, or in their foreheads: and that no man might buy or sell, save he that had the mark, or the name of the beast, or the number of his name.” (Revelation 13:16–17)
This mark, which the antichrist will force all people to receive, will not only be the only means of buying and selling; it will also be responsible for the eternal loss of the soul:
“And the third angel followed them, saying with a loud voice, If any man worship the beast and his image, and receive his mark in his forehead, or in his hand, the same shall drink of the wine of the wrath of God, which is poured out without mixture into the cup of his indignation; and he shall be tormented with fire and brimstone in the presence of the holy angels, and in the presence of the Lamb: and the smoke of their torment ascendeth up for ever and ever: and they have no rest day nor night, who worship the beast and his image, and whosoever receiveth the mark of his name.” (Revelation 14:9–11)

It seems, then, that the mark has such an effect on those who receive it that there is no longer any possibility of repentance. That is, receiving the mark amounts to willingly handing over a person’s free will to the antichrist, and so to Satan. Exactly how the will is to be controlled we do not know, nor could anyone have imagined it in all the centuries since the book of Revelation was written. But today, in the year 2018, new technology is showing that such a thing is possible in the not-too-distant future.
“And when these things begin to come to pass, then look up, and lift up your heads; for your redemption draweth nigh.” (Luke 21:28)

Dear readers, let us make a firm decision to follow Jesus Christ with all our heart, and to get ready, because the Lord is coming!

“And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares. For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth. Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.” (Luke 21:34–36)!
“He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.” (Revelation 22:20).
