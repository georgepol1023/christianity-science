“But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee…” (Part 1)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

June 2017 · The “Christianity & Science” team

“…and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee: or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee: and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee. Who knoweth not in all these that the hand of the LORD hath wrought this? In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind.” (Job 12:7–10)

For the honest person who observes and studies nature, it becomes obvious that the wonderful mechanisms found everywhere in our surroundings bear witness to the existence of a creating Mind that designed and built everything. In this feature we will mention only a few of the countless signs of God’s wisdom in the animal kingdom. These are mechanisms that people even try to imitate in various technologies (biomimetics).

Bats use echolocation to find their way, avoid obstacles and locate their prey. That is, they send out high-frequency sounds and then use the echo produced when the sound waves are reflected off surfaces. It is essentially a sonar system, like the one on modern ships, which uses the spread of sound waves through the sea to detect and identify other vessels and various objects, and also to map and scan the sea bed.

The migration instinct of birds is also remarkable. When winter comes they migrate to warmer climates, and in spring they return home. To migrate, birds must know at least three things: the coordinates of where they are, of their destination, and the route they will take. During their journey they fly either under a cloudy sky, so that they cannot see landmarks in the sky; or above the clouds, so that they cannot see landmarks on the ground; or even inside the clouds, where there are no visible signs at all. So to navigate, birds use many cues in combination, such as the position of the sun and the stars, the direction of the prevailing winds and even smells from the environment. Pigeons, indeed, have a developed system of biomagnets with which they sense the earth’s magnetic field and the effect of the earth’s rotation on its axis, and so can correct their course.

Loggerhead turtles (Caretta caretta), from the moment they hatch, cross thousands of kilometres of open ocean on their migrations, finding their way without visual aids. It has been found that they can sense, on the one hand, the changing strength of the earth’s magnetic field, which is stronger near the poles and weaker at the equator (so they “know” their latitude), and, on the other, the different inclination of the field relative to the earth’s surface – the constantly changing angle at which the magnetic field lines cross the earth’s surface (so they “know” their longitude). According to the researchers, every point in the ocean has its own slightly different “magnetic signature”, and so the loggerhead in effect follows “magnetic coordinates” on its way across the oceans. It is a “biological GPS”!

Another species that travels very long distances to breed is the salmon. Indeed, each new generation returns to lay its eggs in exactly the same rivers as the previous generation! The salmon migrates thousands of kilometres from the ocean to its spawning grounds, travelling against the current of the rivers. Then it goes back to the ocean, to return again the next year. The young that hatch swim off to the ocean, to return just once to exactly the place where they were born, this time to breed. This kind of fish, too, finds its way by magnetic navigation, built into its body and matched to the “magnetic signature” of the places where salmon will be born, live and breed.

According to the theory of evolution, these mechanisms arose as the result of blind chance, when at some point a “random” migration of thousands of kilometres happened to turn out well. So we realise how naive such a view is, and it is being refuted by modern scientific discoveries.

“O LORD, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.” (Psalm 104:24)

The Person through whom all things were made, the Bible reveals to us, is Jesus Christ, the Son and Word of God, who not only created us but also secures the eternal future, through His sacrifice on the cross and His resurrection, of every person who repents of his sins and calls on His name.

“All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.” (John 1:3)
