Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

“Knowledge shall be increased” (Part 1)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

December 2019 · The “Christianity & Science” team

“But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.” (Daniel 12:4)

One of the “signs of the last days”, which also signals the imminent coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, is the very rapid – we might say explosive – increase of knowledge. With the power and speed of computer systems as its undisputed engine, the train of human discoveries is racing wildly towards… the cliff edge! We have often said that we are in no way against human achievements. After all, God placed within man the mental abilities required: “And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness” (Genesis 1:26).

But this knowledge not only cannot lead man to the goal he should be seeking (the salvation of his soul); on the contrary, it draws him away from it, because “knowledge puffeth up, but charity edifieth” (1 Corinthians 8:1).

CALCULATING MACHINES

From ancient times, as people tried to improve their way of life, they began to develop the arts and letters. The discovery of mathematics played an important part. But as the complexity of calculations grew, so did the need for a machine that could carry out correctly, in a short time, calculations for which a person would need whole hours or perhaps days.

From antiquity until the 1930s – that is, for more than about 2,500 years – people used simple calculating tools such as the counting board or abacus. In the 1950s calculating machines appeared (the 1st generation of computers). Then within only a few years transistors and integrated circuits took over. The number of transistors placed on a microprocessor doubles every 18 months (Moore’s law). The key feature is the high speed of carrying out simple mathematical operations (additions and multiplications), through which all the functions of the computer are performed (4th generation). The 5th generation, with the microprocessor (1971), reaches even higher speeds. And in November 2019 the launch of 6th-generation computers was announced, marking a crucial change in the relationship between people and computers…

The race for ever faster and more powerful systems is relentless. The winner will prevail over the others. The society of knowledge and information technology (see the next issue) is spreading everywhere!

In June 2018 IBM’s new supercomputer Summit was unveiled, able to carry out 200 million billion calculations a second. The specifications of this machine defy reason. As the New York Times put it, a person would need 63 billion years to do what this computer can do in one second! Its reign was short. While the ink on this article is not yet dry, a well-known company has announced a quantum computer with incredible capabilities. A problem that Summit would solve in 10,000 years (by projected calculation), the quantum computer needs only 200 seconds for…

Sadly, in the world of “smartphones” and the dizzying speeds of supercomputers and quantum computers, there is no room for the human soul. You will never hear of forgiveness and of eternal life. You will not hear of our Lord Jesus Christ, or of His blood that was shed on the cross of Golgotha. In the world of unimaginable speeds and artificial intelligence there is no room for the God who gave us all this indescribable material and spiritual wealth that we are trying to decode. Man, at the height of his pride, is spiritually bankrupt. “Where is the wise? where is the scribe? where is the disputer of this world? hath not God made foolish the wisdom of this world? For after that in the wisdom of God the world by wisdom knew not God, it pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe.” (1 Corinthians 1:20–21)

“Who is a wise man and endued with knowledge among you? let him shew out of a good conversation his works with meekness of wisdom. But if ye have bitter envying and strife in your hearts, glory not, and lie not against the truth. This wisdom descendeth not from above, but is earthly, sensual, devilish. For where envying and strife is, there is confusion and every evil work.” (James 3:13–16)

We dare not claim that these things are rare in a world with so much knowledge. Sadly they make the newspaper headlines every day! Humanity as a whole has rejected the true God and embraced the idols of knowledge falsely so called. The existence of the Creator God, an understanding of His holiness and righteous judgement, eternal life and eternal loss in hell, are met with a faint, condescendingly ironic smile!

Let us return (the invitation is personal) to the wisdom from above that God gives so generously, which is “…first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy. And the fruit of righteousness is sown in peace of them that make peace.” (James 3:17–18)
