Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

The wonder of the human body!

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

February 2024 · The “Christianity & Science” team

Modern man looks for miracles before he will believe that the Creator God exists. “If I saw a real miracle in front of me, then I would believe too!” he cries arrogantly. Others claim they can only believe in something they can touch, study and experiment with.

But as Augustine said:

“And men go abroad to admire the heights of mountains, the mighty waves of the sea, the broad tides of rivers, the compass of the ocean, and the circuits of the stars, and pass themselves by.”

The human body is a wonder in itself: a masterpiece of engineering, biology, physics and every science. From one original cell, the zygote, through cell division after cell division, the human body is formed, made up of trillions of cells with different shapes and functions. And yet they all work together in harmony to carry out the functions needed.

Cells that are alike in form and take part in the same function make up a tissue. Different tissues in turn work together to form an organ, and finally many organs work together to form an organ system; and all the systems together make up the human body.

From the microscopic world, where DNA in the nucleus of the cells “directs” the making of proteins, to the larger world, where the heart muscle beats ceaselessly like a perfect four-chambered mechanical pump, the wonderful design is plain to see, and the wisdom within it holds even the most sceptical spellbound.

Just think:

In the brain there are about 100 billion neurons, whose nerve fibres have a total length of about 2,000,000 km. We could wrap them around our Earth 50 times…

The heart beats about 100,000 times a day and pumps 13,000 litres of blood. And it does this tirelessly every day for the whole of our lives.

In the human lungs there are 700 million alveoli, with a total surface area of 70 m², about the area of a tennis court.

King David, without the knowledge of physiology and anatomy we have today, but by the revelation of the Holy Spirit, says in Psalm 139:14:

“I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works;
and that my soul knoweth right well.”

So if you are looking today for a miracle of the Creator God’s love, you have one in front of you. It is you! The One who made you with such love and wisdom longs for a daily relationship of love with you, because He made you for one unique purpose…

“…for I have created him for my glory, I have formed him; yea, I have made him.”

(Isaiah 43:7)
