Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

Family time in the age of technology (Part 2)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

August 2020 · The “Christianity & Science” team

“Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god…” (Psalm 16:4)

It would be unfair at this point not to mention again the benefits of the development of science and its applications through technology. Until a few decades ago, for example, a letter to a loved one working abroad might take a month to arrive. Now there are countless apps through which you can talk to them instantly, in real time, and see them; and soon, with holograms, you will find it hard to tell the real from the virtual.

It seems that technology has an answer for all your “needs”… Do you feel lonely? No problem. The latest video game is so addictive that you won’t want to “come out” of it. But when the lights come on, you will be alone again, hopelessly alone.

Do you feel anger, rage, hatred, because – rightly or wrongly – you were insulted or mistreated at work or at school? No problem… In the latest video game the blood flows in rivers. Grab your weapon and charge at them. Let your passions run wild at the enemy’s suggestions (you’re the best at killing, too…), and let the violence that has built up inside you fill the screen with bodies. And if you are fatally wounded, so what? There’s the reset button. Everything starts again from the beginning… Or does it?

Thousands of studies confirm it: violence breeds violence. The countless cases of mass killings in schools and in everyday life, by blind instruments deceived by the devil and Satan, are a tragic confirmation. Surging emotions that cannot be expressed, because of the poverty of language these media bring, try violently to be heard through riots. The state’s forces of repression grow stronger. Democracy is in danger.

Sex is presented as a romantic pastime and a right. Purity is despised and mocked. But the consequences are hidden: psychological disorders, unwanted pregnancies. The structure of the family, as God Himself defined it, is officially trampled on, and people in prominent positions… “declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have rewarded evil unto themselves.” (Isaiah 3:9).

Diligent work, the joy of creating, the reward of honest toil are now presented as old-fashioned. Their place is taken by the “quick grab”, easy profit, the passing thing.

The terrible arsenal keeps growing and becoming more and more attractive to the carnal (and not only the carnal) person: television, smartphones, tablets, laptops and popcorn, on the sofa or in the armchair. That, after all, is (usually) what the older generations do too (even grandparents). The term “toxic family” is heard more and more often.

Tools that are useful up to a point can, if they are left to work unchecked within the family, prove fatal to the survival of the whole family.

“But,” someone will say, “we have to be up to date!” The poor children in particular get bored and restless if they are not kept busy with something, even for a little while… anything at all. Here we should note that boredom is one of the states through which children are helped to develop virtues such as imagination and creativity. You will see a bored child suddenly roll up his sleeves and turn a reed into a pipe or a wooden box into a tree house. In the process he learns to ask questions (of any adult who is really available!), to open books and encyclopaedias. From a will-less victim of other people’s purposes, he blossoms and bears fruit. One of our team (Christianity & Science) remembers spending whole days studying, with his father, the surface tension of liquids and solids. Their tools: a bowl of water and a few small pieces of plastic. The father was there – willing, available to play along with his child.

The purpose of this trilogy, which ends (?) in the next issue, is of course not to exhaust such an important subject. Tens of thousands of thorough studies have been written. But we believe we have something special to give. The land where the apostles taught, and which the Holy Spirit used so powerfully, deserves something better. Let us be ready for the Rapture of the Church!

The “Christianity & Science” team

Published in the team's column in the newspaper “Christianity”, August 2020.
