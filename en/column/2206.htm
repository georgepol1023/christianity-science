Our column in the newspaper “Christianity”

Confusion about the two sexes, and their different roles! (Part 1)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

December 2022 · The “Christianity & Science” team

Our age is marked by frightening confusion. There is a widespread questioning of all established and commonly accepted values, which leads in the end to absurdity and not to fruitful dialogue, as in the past. We have reached the point where what defines a human being is up for discussion, and whether there is a difference between biological sex and gender. As a result, society has to answer absurd questions, and in the end to reap what it has sown for so many years.

Recently the members of a girls’ volleyball team in the USA became the target of the local community (and beyond) because they objected to a trans student using the same changing room 1. This was because the law in force allows every student to use the changing room that he feels best matches his own gender…

A society that, wanting to celebrate difference, in the end does exactly the opposite. It flattens everything. It does not distinguish between different and equal. For of course before God we are all equal, but at the same time so beautifully complementary and different from one another. “There is neither Jew nor Greek, there is neither bond nor free, there is neither male nor female: for ye are all one in Christ Jesus.” (Galatians 3:28).

There is no difference in human worth, in eternal salvation and in access to the throne of grace. There are different God-given roles!

And in the end the perversion grows worse when men and women abandon their sexual identity and try to take on the characteristics of the opposite biological sex. The Word of God in 1 Corinthians 6:9–10 points out to us: “Be not deceived: neither fornicators, nor idolaters, nor adulterers, nor effeminate, nor abusers of themselves with mankind… shall inherit the kingdom of God.” “Effeminate” (in Greek, malakos) describes a man who by choice takes on the characteristics of the female sex and identifies himself as a woman in every expression of his life.

The amazing thing, though, is that, contrary to what they want us to believe, a person can be set free when he calls on the blood of the Lord Jesus Christ and the power of the Holy Spirit.

---------------------------------

For those who struggle with gender dysphoria, the answer lies in letting the Holy Spirit change their heart, not letting the doctor change their sex!

---------------------------------

“And such were some of you: but ye are washed, but ye are sanctified, but ye are justified in the name of the Lord Jesus, and by the Spirit of our God.” (1 Corinthians 6:11)

In the end, today’s gender confusion is simply one of the many symptoms of a bigger problem in our society. It is man’s refusal of God’s absolute authority! A society that takes the laws of its Creator as bonds and chains and wants to break them… “Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.” (Psalm 2:3)

How beautifully, from the beginning, “God created man, in the likeness of God made he him; male and female created he them; and blessed them…” (Genesis 5:1–2). Blessing and joy in life are not found in breaking God’s laws but in obeying them and keeping them. The dysphoria is not in the sex but in the conscience, which keeps crying out to a person to return to the Father’s house.

Today the Lord’s invitation can be heard more loudly than ever:

“O that thou hadst hearkened to my commandments! then had thy peace been as a river, and thy righteousness as the waves of the sea.” (Isaiah 48:18)

God willing, in Part 2 we will look through the Bible at these different roles of man and woman in many areas of everyday life, and once again we will cry out with the psalmist:

“O LORD, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all.” (Psalm 104:24)
