The origin of all things! | Part 2: The riddle of information, and time as a challenge for the origin of life

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

August 2026 · The “Christianity & Science” team

In the previous article, drawing on the recent discussion published online under the title “The Origin of Everything”1, we saw that modern cosmology gives strong evidence that the universe had a beginning, and that the physical constants appear remarkably finely tuned to allow life to exist. The next question is even more personal: how did life itself appear?

In 1952 Stanley Miller and Harold Urey carried out one of the best-known experiments in the history of science. They tried to recreate the conditions of the early Earth and to show how the building blocks of life could have formed. Their results caused great excitement.

At around the same time, however, another discovery was about to change our understanding of life radically. Watson and Crick revealed the structure of DNA. Since then science has found that DNA is not simply a chemical molecule but a system for storing and passing on information of astonishing complexity.

Scientists stress that the problem of the origin of life is not only about producing the chemical components of a cell. The real question is how all these parts were organised into a working whole.

James Tour uses a simple example: if we take a car engine apart down to the very last screw, we would not expect the parts to put themselves back together, even if we shook them for a very long time. The difficulty lies not only in making the parts but above all in organising them and connecting them so that they work.

There is also the well-known problem of interdependence. DNA needs the cell in order to be read and to function, while the cell needs DNA in order to be built and maintained. Each one presupposes the other.

The idea of information is of particular interest. When we come across a message, a book, computer code or an organised system of symbols, we know from everyday experience that there is a mind behind it. That is why Bill Gates remarked that DNA is like a computer program, only far more complex than anything man has ever created.

In the search for extraterrestrial intelligence, scientists examine radio signals, looking for organised information. The presence of information is taken as a sign of intelligence. The same question arises about the biological information we find inside every living cell.

Genesis 1:3

“And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.”

The creating Mind speaks a Word, and it comes into being!

Finally, the speakers point out something that is often overlooked. Many people think that time solves the problem of the origin of life. In reality, time usually works the other way. Everything tends to wear out and break down. RNA molecules break apart quickly, proteins have a limited lifespan, and every organised system needs constant maintenance.

The Bible describes a world that is subject to decay. At the same time, though, it testifies that behind life stands the Creator. As we read in Acts 17:28, “For in him we live, and move, and have our being.”

Science continues to investigate the great questions. Yet the more we discover the complexity of the universe and of life, the more many researchers find that reality seems to reflect not blind indifference but order, reason and purpose: a Creator God, whose character is revealed very beautifully in the pages of the Bible.

“For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse.” (Romans 1:20)
