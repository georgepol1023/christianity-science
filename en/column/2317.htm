How man imitates biological processes

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

October 2024 · The “Christianity & Science” team

Biomimetics is the term used to describe the technological achievements by which people imitate the patterns and designs of nature, especially in robotics, artificial intelligence, medicine, materials technology (nanotechnology) and architecture.

From the beginning of technology, man has used nature as a source of inspiration to solve his everyday problems. He imitated the beaks of birds to make the points of spears, the spiral shape of shells to make the screw, the architecture of certain natural structures to build buildings that use less energy, and the shape of birds’ wings to be able to fly.

He realised that in all biological systems there is awe-inspiring wisdom and a built-in design worth imitating. Plants, animals and all biological structures are not designed by chance: behind them lies a divine Mind – a wisdom worthy of man’s imitation.

Job, who was a contemporary of Abraham and lived around 2000 BC, had perceived the greatness of the wisdom in biological systems, and exclaims by the Holy Spirit: “But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee: or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee: and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee. Who knoweth not in all these that the hand of the LORD hath wrought this?” (Job 12:7–9)

Let us look, then, at some examples of how man has brought into his everyday life technologies he has copied by observing and studying the divine design in nature.

  • The most typical example in everyday use, whose creator was inspired by nature, is Velcro, which is used instead of zips or laces on clothes, shoes and much else. It was invented by the Swiss engineer George de Mestral in 1941. His inspiration came while he was trying to remove burrs (plants usually found in fields) from his dog’s fur and his knitted jumper. As he could not remove them easily, he decided to put them under the microscope to understand exactly what was happening. What he realised was that two separate products, made of hooks and loops, can be joined together and are hard to pull apart.
  • The Eastgate Centre is a shopping centre and office complex in Harare, Zimbabwe, designed to be ventilated and cooled by entirely natural means. It opened in 1996 and contains 5,600 m² of shops, 26,000 m² of office space and parking for 450 cars. The building is modelled on the “skyscrapers” of termites and the system by which they keep a constant temperature inside through the chimney at the top. Using the same system, the building has chimneys that take in the cool air at night, which is then released through the floor slabs throughout the day. As a result, less energy is used to keep the inside of the building cool.
  • The sharks of the Galápagos Islands, although they do not move fast, do not have a single bacterium on the surface of their bodies and show no infection. The main reason is the toothed shape of their scales. The American company Sharklet™, studying this unusual structure, made an antibacterial covering that is very useful in hospitals. This technology is truly revolutionary and can reduce to a minimum the medicines given and the infections caught in hospital during patients’ stays.
  • Bats have a remarkable natural ability, in spite of their weak eyesight, to move easily and very fast in the dark. Bats use waves sent out from their larynx, which are reflected off the objects around them. In this way they find their way perfectly in the dark, avoiding obstacles and locating their prey. This discovery helped in building detection devices such as radar and sonar, which work by the reflection of waves (electromagnetic or sound). This is how ships, for example, map the sea bed and check whether there are reefs or other places dangerous to the ship.

Sadly, many scientists try to attribute this wonderful wisdom of design to supposed millions of years of evolutionary processes. They stubbornly refuse to see the truth that is plain in front of them. They refuse to see that behind every wise design there is a wonderful mind. “Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools”, and turned their backs on the Creator God.

“For after that in the wisdom of God the world by wisdom knew not God, it pleased God by the foolishness of preaching to save them that believe.” (1 Corinthians 1:21)
