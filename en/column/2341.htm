Advice for teenagers (Part 2)

Translated from the Greek original in the newspaper “Christianity”. Read it in Greek

February 2025 · The “Christianity & Science” team

As we said at length in our previous article, the teenage years are one of the most productive and important periods in a person’s life. Habits are formed, outlooks are settled, character is built and knowledge takes root.

That is why the Word of God gives very beautiful and vital advice to teenagers. We saw that the Lord wants a young person to be joyful, and that this joy comes from communion with Him; and also how important it is for a young person to be “redeeming the time”, not wasting it on useless and harmful habits.

Let us look now at three more pieces of advice that the Word of God gives to teenagers.

Your body belongs to God:

There is a mistaken idea that our body belongs to us, so we can do whatever we like with it. This greatly influences the way young people in the world dress, cut their hair and wear make-up. In recent years many young people have even marked their bodies permanently with tattoos and pierced them with various pieces of jewellery.

The Word of God, however, advises us: “What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own? For ye are bought with a price: therefore glorify God in your body, and in your spirit, which are God’s.” (1 Corinthians 6:19–20)

The born-again young person, in whom the Holy Spirit dwells, is called to glorify God with his body too, without giving in to the dictates of fashion and peer pressure. That is why the apostle Paul urges “that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind…” (Romans 12:1–2)

God’s plans are higher than yours:

For a young person who is gradually finishing secondary school and heading towards adulthood, it is perfectly natural to dream about and plan his future. Which direction will he take, which course would he like to get into, which profession will give him financial independence, and what would he like his family to be like in the future? These and many other questions trouble the mind of a young person who has to make decisions and plan the next steps of his life.

In the middle of all this, though, he must not forget that “…my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the LORD.” (Isaiah 55:8)

God has a wonderful and perfect plan for His children, and calls all of us to explore it in prayer and to walk in it. He wants the best for His children, so that He may be glorified to the full in their lives.

So, young person, as you make your plans, do not forget to seek God’s counsel and, as King Solomon urges us: “Trust in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.”

Love the Word of God:

The source of all wisdom and knowledge, and the strength to walk in them, is found in the Word of God.

The Lord, speaking to Joshua, urges him: “This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.” (Joshua 1:8).

And speaking to the people, Moses reminds them: “…it is not a vain thing for you; because it is your life” (Deuteronomy 32:47)

And finally the apostle Paul, speaking with love to young Timothy, tells him: “Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all.” (1 Timothy 4:15).

So start today to study the Word of God every day, ready to obey it, and you will see progress and growth in your life!
