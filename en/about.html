Who we are

Welcome to christianity-science.gr

Through the latest discoveries, and by looking closely at the wonders of Creation that modern science is uncovering, we want to make it clear that the Bible is the Word of God and the absolute Truth.

The purpose of this website is not simply to offer a body of knowledge, but through that knowledge to glorify the name of the Lord and to lift up the Cross of Christ and His redeeming work, so that everyone is moved to seek and to know their Creator.

Behind the great discoveries we should see the Creator. Man tends to worship nature, but worship and honour belong to the One who created it. Those who worship the creature more than the Creator (Romans 1:25) fall into corruption, and the truth escapes them (Romans 1:21). When a person finds the truth, their life is made right and complete.

It is true that someone who enjoys the benefits of science without knowing the Creator misses a very large part of the good they could receive. For what God wants to give us is not only what comes to us through creation: His purpose is also to heal the soul and to give it eternal life.

That God is the source of all knowledge (see also Exodus 31:3) is a great truth that is either overlooked through carelessness or deliberately set aside. The Lord gives man science so that he may know the things He has made, with the final purpose that man may know God.

What this website does not set out to do:

  • It does not try to offer people a faith built on reason and scientific knowledge.
  • It does not aim to present scientific theories and opinions in detail.
  • It does not try to force the Bible to fit current scientific theories.

What this website does set out to do:

  • It seeks to tear down the views that hide behind pseudo-scientific knowledge in order to damage the credibility of the Bible.

    “Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ.” (2 Corinthians 10:5)
  • We want to shake the weak foundations of those scientific views that are published uncritically in the media every day and stand against the Word of God, by offering plenty of scientific arguments of our own.
  • We want to encourage people to think differently about what they may be taught at school, or hear every day, about the relationship between Christianity and science.
  • We want to confirm and strengthen Christians' faith in the inerrancy of the Word of God, and that it is given by inspiration of God (2 Timothy 3:16).
  • We want to give Christians, and especially Christian students, arguments so that they do not hesitate to take part in such discussions.

We should point out, however, that we do not present ourselves as scientific authorities who can answer every question, but as people redeemed by Jesus Christ who, by talking and working together with you, will try to grow in knowledge and in faith in the Word of God and in the redeeming work of the Cross of Golgotha.

Finally, we would like to stress that scientific knowledge helps, but we always come to God by faith (Hebrews 11:3 & 6). It is there that the Word of God is proved true and that God does His work in a person's life.

After all, the Word of God does not need the support of science to do the work for which God sent it. Yet the Christian faith has a foundation that also rests on reasoned arguments. Today, when knowledge has increased (Daniel 12:4), God calls us to love Him with all our mind as well.

“… The Lord our God is one Lord: and thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind, and with all thy strength: this is the first commandment.” (Mark 12:29–30)

We hope you enjoy exploring our website. We ask for your prayers and your involvement: send us questions on subjects that concern you, and any related material you may have. You can reach us by e-mail at science@christianity.gr


The Lord bless you,
