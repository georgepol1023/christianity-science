# English wording guide

The English version follows the voice and terminology of the sibling site
**facplondon.org.uk** (Free Apostolic Church of Pentecost – London), so both sites read as one family.
This guide records the conventions; it does not copy that site's text.

## Language and tone
- **British English** spelling and usage: *programme*, *colour*, *centre*, *recognised*; dates as *15 October 2026*; times as *22:00* (Greek time).
- **Warm and direct**, addressing the reader as *you*. Short sentences, plain words.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons (*Messages & videos*, not *Messages & Videos*). Keep the `&` where the Greek uses it.

## Bible quotations
- Quote the **King James Version (KJV)**, which is public domain. FACP uses KJV for its verse of the day.
- Do **not** translate a Greek Bible quotation ourselves, and do not quote copyrighted versions (NKJV, NIV, ESV) beyond brief references.
- References: book, chapter and verse, e.g. *John 8:32*, *2 Timothy 3:16*, *1 Peter 5:7*. Greek ordinals (Α΄, Β΄) become 1, 2.
- The site motto, Ιωάννης η΄ 32: *“And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.”* (John 8:32, KJV)

## What is not translated
- The audio (always marked **Audio in Greek**).
- PDFs, and pages that are copies of other publishers' articles: these link to the Greek original.
- Proper names of people keep their usual Latin spelling (e.g. *Christos Lanitis*).
