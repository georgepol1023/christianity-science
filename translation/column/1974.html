“All these are the beginning of sorrows”

These words are part of the answer our Lord Jesus Christ gave to His disciples’ question: “what shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?” (Matthew 24:3).

In the last few years all of us have witnessed the rapidly approaching disruption of the environment. A host of interdependent worldwide changes are taking place that cry out that the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ to receive His church is near. The closer we come to the end of this age (the times of the Gentiles), the more events will follow one another with increasing frequency and intensity, like the birth pains of a pregnant woman that lead to the birth. How accurate the Word of God is! The Lord said: “Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.” (Matthew 24:35).

The fires in California, for example, were still raging when fires broke out in Siberia, Alaska and even the Antarctic! Straight afterwards came the fires in the virgin forests of the Amazon, and since September the planet has watched, thunderstruck, what has been called the greatest ecological disaster of the 21st century: the devastating fires in Australia!

There have always been forest fires, and indeed this is one of the ways forests are renewed. But never with such intensity and frequency.

As these lines are written, an area twice the size of Belgium has already burned, and human lives and property have been lost, as well as more than one and a half billion animals. In reality the danger to health appears after the disaster is over. When the smoke and the suspended particles reach the upper atmosphere and fall back to earth as rain, they will soak into the lakes, the sea and the surface waters – that is, the water we will drink! There is no way to replace Australia’s lost wealth.

More than 700 specialist scientists have stated that all this will get worse unless the emission of the gases that cause the greenhouse effect is quickly reduced. The scientists are categorical: “Climate change exists, and man is the cause! What is more, we do not expect ‘better days’; the situation will get worse very quickly!

We are hearing a bell! Australia is the first appearance, on such a scale, of a coming flood tide of climate events that tomorrow will strike America, China, Africa, Europe – no one knows where. But we are talking about the beginning.”

In the middle of all this, the world’s silence about the real owner of the whole universe, our Lord Jesus Christ, is deafening (Psalm 24). “…For by him (Jesus Christ) were all things created, that are in heaven… and he is before all things, and by him all things consist.” (Colossians 1:16–17)

Science, then, agrees with the Word of God, which thousands of years ago described prophetically what is happening around us. It reveals to man the real cause and urges each one to return to God.

“The earth mourneth and fadeth away, the world languisheth and fadeth away, the haughty people of the earth do languish. The earth also is defiled under the inhabitants thereof; because they have transgressed the laws, changed the ordinance, broken the everlasting covenant.” (Isaiah 24:4–5).

Sadly, humanity as a whole will continue, unrepentant and deceived, on the road to destruction. The invitation is personal. Let us return to God, washed in the blood of Jesus Christ.
