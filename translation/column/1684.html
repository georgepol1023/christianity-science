Scientific references in the Bible

In many places in the Bible there are statements that fall within the scope of the sciences (for example physics, astronomy, medicine and others).

In these statements, despite the knowledge of the time, the words of the Bible prove correct and consistent with modern scientific knowledge, which confirms that what is written is given by inspiration of God. Let us look at some examples:

The number of the stars:

“… I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven” – Genesis 22:17

“As the host of heaven cannot be numbered, neither the sand of the sea measured: so will I multiply the seed of David my servant, and the Levites that minister unto me.” – Jeremiah 33:22

The number of grains of sand on the whole Earth is estimated at between 1020 and 1024. The number of stars in the universe is estimated at 1021 or 1022. These numbers cannot be counted, just as the prophet Jeremiah says. So we see that the orders of magnitude the Bible chooses to compare are neither random nor an exaggeration, but mathematically comparable. And this statement was made at a time when there were no telescopes, and with the naked eye at most about 3,000 stars could be seen in the night sky.

The Earth is suspended, and it is round:

“He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.” – Job 26:7

“Have ye not known? have ye not heard? hath it not been told you from the beginning? have ye not understood from the foundations of the earth? It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers” – Isaiah 40:21–22

These verses are in complete contrast with most of the cosmological ideas of the time, which held that the Earth is flat, and found it unthinkable that it should not rest on something.

It is worth noting that the book of Job is dated to the 15th century BC. The book of the prophet Isaiah was written in the 8th century BC – that is, about 4 centuries before Aristotle put forward the view that the Earth may be spherical.

Human flesh is made from the dust of the ground:

“And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.” – Genesis 2:7

“In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground; for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return.” – Genesis 3:19

The elements of the human body are oxygen, carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, calcium, phosphorus, sulphur, potassium, chlorine, sodium and other minerals.

All of these elements are found in the soil of the earth.

Underground “oceans”:

Since December 2014, studies by international scientific teams have put forward well-founded claims for the existence of an underground zone of water a few kilometres deep in our planet’s crust, in quantities comparable to the total amount of water in all the Earth’s oceans!

The confirmation of such a discovery will come as no surprise to faithful Christians, since it will be one more piece of evidence for the reliability of the account of the Flood, according to which huge quantities of water burst out of the interior of the Earth, enough to cover the whole planet:

“In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened. […] And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered.” – Genesis 7:11, 19

The first snakes had legs:

According to finds studied by a team of researchers at Yale University in the USA (published May 2015), the first kinds of snake had small limbs with ankles and toes. Naturally we turn to the relevant verse of Genesis, which, to the surprise of all the doubters, suddenly gains a sound scientific basis:

“And the LORD God said unto the serpent, Because thou hast done this, thou art cursed above all cattle, and above every beast of the field; upon thy belly shalt thou go, and dust shalt thou eat all the days of thy life” – Genesis 3:14

So we find that with the progress of science and the increase of knowledge, the Bible is not disproved but resoundingly confirmed, filling all Christians with joy and increasing their faith in this book, which is the Word of God. So we can agree with the words of the psalmist:

“Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever.”

– Psalm 119:160
