Lessons from time!

One of the most important questions science has had to clarify is the concept of time. Since ancient times thousands of scientists and philosophers have wondered about time as a physical quantity and tried to grasp it. And there are very many questions!

What is time?

Is it simply a built-in property of the universe? Has it always existed? Can we go back in time? Will it stop existing at some point? Is it the same for every observer? Can I measure it objectively, or is it something subjective? In the end, does time exist, or is it simply an illusion?

In this article we will set out some basic properties of time according to science, what the Word of God, which cannot lie, has to tell us, and how through all this we can receive useful teaching for our lives!

Time is created: Whereas people used to believe that the universe is eternal, it has been shown that it had a moment of creation, and so time is something created. Together with energy, space and matter, time too was created.

The apostle Paul tells us that God “… hath … spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds.” (Hebrews 1:2). (The Greek word for “worlds” here also means “ages”.)

Time is relative: Einstein proposed, and experiments later proved, that time dilates: that is, it flows at a different rate depending on the gravitational field we are in and the speed at which we are moving.

Yet Moses, around 1500 BC, wrote in Psalm 90: “For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.” (Psalm 90:4)

The idea of eternity: The highest speed that can be reached in the universe is the speed of light, and whatever moves at that speed is not subject to the wear of time. Also, time affects only what is inside the material universe and not what is outside it. But God Himself, as Creator, is not limited by His creation, as the ancient gods were limited by the wear of time.

The apostle Paul tells us about Jesus Christ: “… who only hath immortality, dwelling in the light which no man can approach unto” (1 Timothy 6:16), and the prophet Isaiah: “For thus saith the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy…” (Isaiah 57:15)

Time flows one way: Unlike the three dimensions of space, time flows only forwards. I cannot go back or change the events that have happened. I cannot reverse the arrow of time, nor enjoy again the youth that has passed.

That is why the Preacher tells us: “To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven” (Ecclesiastes 3:1), and advises us to serve and glorify God now, “while the evil days come not” of old age.

From all this we draw two very important lessons for our lives!

Don’t let the years go by. The past is gone; the future does not belong to you… Today, give your heart to Christ! And learn to redeem every moment (Ephesians 5:16). Make the prayer of Moses your daily priority:

“So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.” (Psalm 90:12)

The moment is coming when God the Father will cry out: “Behold, I make all things new!” (Revelation 21:5). And John bore witness and said: “… I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away” (Revelation 21:1)

In an eternity where there will be no pain, sorrow or tears, “but righteousness, and peace, and joy in the Holy Ghost”.

Go to the Cross of Christ, repent, leave your sin and win eternal life!
