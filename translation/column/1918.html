Our planet is gravely ill: who will save us?

“I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. My help cometh from the LORD, which made heaven and earth.” (Psalm 121:1–2)

With rapidly increasing frequency and intensity, extreme weather is striking the whole planet: uncontrolled wildfires, floods, landslides, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, tsunamis. The Arctic in flames! Alaska and Siberia ringed by fire! In the Antarctic (the South Pole), an area twice the size of Australia, the ice is shrinking dramatically. The Antarctic is not simply a vast “natural fridge” keeping everything permanently frozen; it also plays a decisive part in regulating the world’s climate.

As if all this were not enough, the largest tropical forest, the Amazon, has been burning for several months… The Amazon is considered the lungs of the earth, because it produces 20% of the oxygen and absorbs 25% of the carbon dioxide, the main gas causing the greenhouse effect. It is home to the largest collection of plant and animal species in the world (three million species of plants and animals), and to more than a million indigenous people. These phenomena affect one another like a destructive cascade. The end result: food crisis, hunger, unrest, disease, refugees. The verdict of the scientific organisations admits no dispute: for climate change… man is responsible.

The experts give us just 15 years to save the planet.

Who will save us? Where shall we turn?

We were surprised to read that it is technology that will protect us from all these evils. We learned that the fortunate residents of California will soon have an app on their mobile phones warning them that an earthquake will strike in 5 to 10 seconds. So they will have the chance to take some action – for example, to turn off the gas supply and run outside… We learned that we will build drones able to see through smoke and find people in danger. We are not against technology, but only when it is used rightly, and not as a deceptive idol.

The book of the prophet Isaiah describes exactly this attitude of arrogance and rebellion of a people who had turned away. Because of sin, God allowed the Assyrians to invade northern Israel. Instead of repenting and turning back to the Lord, the inhabitants and their rulers made this arrogant statement against God: “The bricks are fallen down, but we will build with hewn stones: the sycomores are cut down, but we will change them into cedars.” (Isaiah 9:10). Those responsible for leading the people rightly led them astray; indeed they flattered them. We don’t need God, they said; we will manage on our own, and much better too. The Lord in His longsuffering shows them another fire: the lawlessness in man’s heart, which eats a person away, alienates him from God and prepares him for the eternal fire. “For wickedness burneth as the fire: it shall devour the briers and thorns, and shall kindle in the thickets of the forest, and they shall mount up like the lifting up of smoke.” (Isaiah 9:18)

God truly loves man, and has proved it in deed. “For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” (John 3:16).

Even in this difficult hour of confusion and self-deception, the Lord has a bright and glorious plan of salvation. He describes it in the Bible, and it is offered freely to every person who will repent and call on the Name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

In brief: suddenly the Lord will catch up His Church into heaven, giving believers the glorious resurrection body (1 Corinthians 15:51–54, Philippians 3:20–21, 1 Thessalonians 4:13–18). As soon as the church has gone, the antichrist will appear and rule the earth for seven terrible years. There will be great tribulation, such as never was on the earth. At the end of the seven years our Lord Jesus Christ will come from heaven with all the saints and set up His kingdom for a thousand years. The earth will be healed and will enjoy her sabbaths (Ezekiel chapter 47, Isaiah 9:18). After the thousand-year reign we will enter endless eternity. The invitation is personal. The Lord Jesus Christ offers it to you freely from His Cross. Accept it with gratitude. “Look unto me, and be ye saved, all the ends of the earth: for I am God, and there is none else.”
