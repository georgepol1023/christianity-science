Confusion about the two sexes, and their different roles! (Part 2)

In the previous article we saw, by the grace of God, how the widespread questioning of all established and commonly accepted values is leading the modern world into absurdity and confusion about the roles and identity of the two sexes: a society that tried to stay open to what is different while deliberately shutting out Jesus Christ; that saw the Creator’s guidance as chains and not as the only road to real joy.

The feminist movement tried to raise up women by abolishing the different roles and duties of the two sexes. It assumed that different roles mean different worth. But all it achieved was that women lost their God-given identity, and with it their basic purpose.

Let us see, though, what the Word of God says about the different roles of the two sexes in two basic areas of everyday Christian life:

The family: In the Epistle to the Ephesians we read: “Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it” (5:25). And on the other hand: “Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord” (5:22).

Many have taken submission as a sign of inferiority and a lower position. But really, which is harder: to obey, or to sacrifice yourself? To submit, or to love the other as your own body?

And how beautiful it is for a wife to rest with trust in the hands of a husband who loves her unconditionally, in a spirit of self-sacrifice and giving!

The church: The different roles of the two sexes in service in the church come mainly from the following three statements:

“the head of the woman is the man” (1 Corinthians 11:3)

“Adam was first formed, then Eve”

(1 Timothy 2:13)

“Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression” (1 Timothy 2:14)

That a woman is not permitted to teach or to be a pastor in the church (1 Timothy 2:12) does not lower her position; it makes it different.

We have dozens of precious examples of holy women in the New Testament who were of great value through their service in the work of God. Priscilla was a fellow worker of the apostle Paul. Euodias and Syntyche laboured with him. Dorcas served the needs of the saints, and Joanna and Susanna (with other women) ministered to Jesus of their substance. The four daughters of Philip prophesied for the Lord, and Phebe was a servant of the church at Cenchrea.

What finer example than the mother of our Lord Jesus Christ! She knew her place and accepted her mission, but at the same time she knew her limits. With humility, and at great personal cost, she submitted to the holy counsel of God. She brought up the Saviour of the world with grace and with the fear of God. Yet she also knew the limits of her own role, taking the fitting place at the right moment: “His mother saith unto the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it.” (John 2:5).

So it is necessary for us today, too, to listen to the advice of the mother of our Lord, and to do whatever He tells us in the holy Word He left us as a sacred trust.

Different roles, with a common goal and the same worth. “But now are they many members, yet but one body.” (1 Corinthians 12:20)
