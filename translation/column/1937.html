“Knowledge shall be increased” (Part 2)

“Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god…” (Psalm 16:4)

As we saw in our previous feature, the race for ever faster and more powerful systems for handling information is relentless. The winner will prevail over the others. The rapidly changing and disruptive processes of technology and science run through every field of human activity. Carried along by the very rapid growth in the computing power of computers and the explosive increase in the speed of the internet, humanity is pouring out – towards a bright future, some say; towards its own destruction, say others…

“The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass…” (Revelation 1:1)

The dominant demand and goal of the new age is the knowledge society. Knowledge is now the power that shapes the international economy in the post-industrial societies of the 21st century. Welcome to the society of information and of knowledge (“falsely so called”), whose aim is to win competitive advantage internationally through the use of information technology (IT).

But who is able, at the individual level, to handle knowledge that grows geometrically? Certainly not today’s human being, with his so limited abilities. Once humanity accepts the scientifically unfounded lie of evolution, the next step is to work out what comes next. Those who embrace the theory of evolution have no problem accepting that the time has come to redefine what a human being is, and for man himself to steer developments so as to pass into the post-human age.

As Ray Kurzweil – holder of many scientific honours, including the US National Medal of Technology – says, “machine intelligence will soon surpass human intelligence”. “By intelligence,” he says, “I include all the diverse and subtle ways in which humans are intelligent – including musical and artistic aptitude, creativity, physically moving through the world, and even responding to emotion.”

In his book The Age of Spiritual Machines (1998) he predicted that by 2019 a $1,000 computer would have the computing power of the human brain, and that by 2050 the same amount would buy the computing power of all the human brains on the planet. Around 2049 computers would claim to be conscious and would demand full human rights, which would be granted to them. It would be impossible to tell a computer with exceptional artificial intelligence (AI) from a human being. (We should note here that 2019 has come and gone, and the scientist’s prediction has not come anywhere near being fulfilled.) A society of knowledge and information, but sadly completely cut off from the true God and Creator: a society that aims supposedly at knowledge for man’s good, but with contempt for what man really is. It pushes man to the margins in order to create the post-human: a chimera, evolved at will, that will (?) be able to handle ever-growing information and hollow knowledge…

The real Creator, the Lord Jesus Christ, with His bloodstained hands stretched out – undeniable proof of His love – invites man, perhaps for the last time, to enter eternal life through His sacrifice. Almighty God does not despise us; on the contrary, He bears with great joy the crown and title: THE SON OF MAN (Daniel 7:13, Matthew 16:13, Revelation 19:12). “Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: but made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: and being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.” (Philippians 2:5–8)

Soon the Lord is coming! Let us get ready to welcome Him!

“Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.” (Revelation 22:20)
