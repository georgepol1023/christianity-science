The Flood (Part 1)

“… by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water: whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished: but the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.” (2 Peter 3:5–7)

By the grace of the Lord, from this month we begin a short feature on the Flood, an event that took place in the time of Noah and which has from time to time raised many questions and doubts about whether it really happened.

The account is in the book of Genesis, chapters 6 to 8, which we recommend our readers to study as they follow this series of articles. Our aim is to answer, simply, briefly but with scientific clarity, some reasonable questions that arise about the building and dimensions of the ark, how all the kinds of animals fitted into it, and whether there is any evidence that a geomorphological event of such a scale once took place on our planet.

Although, according to the Bible, this event is dated about 5,000 years before our own time, the spiritual messages it sends out are more relevant than ever. The state of humanity shortly before the Flood is described very vividly in the following verses:

“And GOD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And it repented the LORD that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him at his heart. And the LORD said, I will destroy man whom I have created from the face of the earth; both man, and beast, and the creeping thing, and the fowls of the air; for it repenteth me that I have made them.” (Genesis 6:5–7).

Jesus Christ, speaking prophetically, revealed that humanity would again be in the same state shortly before His coming:

“And as it was in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man. They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all.” (Luke 17:26–27).

Yet in the middle of this state of things there was one man, Noah, who with his family followed a different road and way of life:

“Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God. […] And God said unto Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me; for the earth is filled with violence through them; and, behold, I will destroy them with the earth.” (Genesis 6:9, 13).

So the one who chose and dared to be different from the corrupt mind of everyone else, and to remain with his household in holiness and in the will of God, was the one who received word of the coming judgment and instructions for escaping it.

In the same way, in our time, as we live through the last days, the believers who make up the church of Christ have word, through the Word of God, of the coming judgment and of the way of salvation out of it all:

“Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.” (Luke 21:36).
