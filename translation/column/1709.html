Evolution or creation? (Part 3)

“All flesh is not the same flesh: but there is one kind of flesh of men, another flesh of beasts, another of fishes, and another of birds.” (1 Corinthians 15:39)

In our previous feature we wrote about the wonderful DNA molecule (genetic material), which contains all the information needed for the structure and working of every organism. According to the Synthetic Theory of Evolution (neo-Darwinism), the mutations that may happen in the genetic material can, as they build up over long periods of time, lead to the creation of new species.


A mutation is defined as any change in the sequence of the “chemical letters” of DNA, and may be due to errors in copying it or to environmental factors (toxic substances, radiation and so on). But modern knowledge in genetics confirms that mutations cannot work in such a way that an organism evolves into a new species.
First we should point out that, on the one hand, mutations are inherited by offspring only if they affect the reproductive cells (sperm, egg), and on the other, the overwhelming majority of them are harmful (hereditary diseases and cancers are due to mutations).


The remaining, non-harmful mutations either happen to cause no damage, or perhaps give some advantage, but only under very specific conditions and at the expense of some other normal function (for example, carriers of the mutation that causes sickle-cell anaemia cannot be infected by malaria). So in reality there are no favourable mutations that can improve an organism and lead to the formation of a new species.
Mathematically, too, the probability of just 4 non-harmful mutations happening, each of which favours the result of the one before, is 1 in 10 octillion (1028). That would need an unimaginable number of cells, which does not even exist on our planet; and in any case just 4 mutations are very far from turning an organism into a different species.


Another important subject is the “biogenetic law”, or “law of recapitulation”, formulated at the end of the 19th century by the German biologist Ernst Haeckel. According to this law, “ontogeny recapitulates phylogeny”: that is, an embryo, as it develops during pregnancy (ontogeny), passes in summary through all the stages of the evolution of its species (phylogeny). For example, the human embryo at first is a single-celled organism (like an amoeba), then shows some folds like gills (the “fish” stage), then has a tail, later looks like a little ape, and finally takes on human form.


To support his theory, Haeckel published drawings of the embryos of various animals and of man, setting out the different stages of development side by side to show the similarities and support the descent of all species from a common ancestor. But it was very soon revealed that this was a great fraud, since the drawings had been falsified – something that Haeckel himself was forced to admit publicly.


As for the human embryo in particular, we should say that the fertilised egg is a cell completely different from a single-celled organism like an amoeba. Also, the folds that are seen have nothing to do with fish gills; they are bone-like structures from which various organs, such as the jaws and the larynx, will be formed. The embryo’s “tail” is simply a projecting part that disappears as the embryo’s body grows, and has no similarity to the formation and function of an animal’s tail. Finally, real images show that the human embryo at no stage of its development looks like a “little ape”.


To our sorrow, however, we see that although Haeckel’s theory collapsed as early as 1910, parts of it can still be found today in various textbooks, even in Greek schools.
The Word of God warns us against every spirit of error that tries to devalue man by putting him on a level with the animals, whereas in reality he is “the image of God”, for whose sake the Son of God shed His blood on the Cross and then rose again and ascended into heaven, to open the way of salvation and eternal life for every person.


“Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.” (Colossians 2:8)
