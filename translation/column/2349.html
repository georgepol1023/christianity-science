The search for intelligence and the meaning of information (Part 2)

In our previous feature we looked at the question of how we can tell whether a structure or formation is the product of chance or the work of intelligence. We gave some examples from which the following logical conclusion follows: random natural processes can produce either chaotic complexity or, at best, certain patterns, but these serve no purpose.

On the other hand, wherever we see complexity that is specified, and formations that serve a purpose, there information is contained, and that is a sure sign that we are looking at the product of an intelligent being.

The structure with perhaps the strongest sign of having been created by intelligence, precisely because it clearly contains information, is found in living organisms: it is their genetic material, DNA. The theory of evolution, pioneered by Darwin’s work in the middle of the 19th century, held that the various species arose from random gradual changes through natural selection, as we have set out in detail in earlier articles. But about a century later, Watson and Crick’s discovery of the role of DNA showed that the way all organisms are built, and all their functions, are determined in every detail by “instructions” that are “written” in this macromolecule.

The structure of DNA shows sequences in which four specific nitrogen-containing chemical bases (adenine, thymine, guanine, cytosine) alternate, working like a chemical “alphabet” of four chemical “letters”. In the sequences of these four “letters”, then, are “stored” or “recorded”, as we might say, the building instructions for the corresponding cell machinery, so that it forms the proteins which in turn precisely determine all the characteristics of the organism and the way it works.

The human DNA in each cell is about 2 metres long, yet thanks to an ingenious packing mechanism it fits into the nucleus of a cell a few millionths of a metre across! Its storage capacity is so efficient that 1 gram of DNA corresponds to more than 200,000,000 GB of storage space. And if we went into the details of the cell machinery involved, we would discover whole chemical “factories” working in complete harmony and coordination with one another.

We want to stress, then, that DNA contains organised information. For it to come about through blind, random processes would be like the wind and the waves forming on a sandy beach not merely a few ripples or a few letters, but the whole of the Encyclopaedia Britannica! If such a volume of organised information arrived on Earth from space, it would be considered undeniable proof that extraterrestrial intelligence exists. But when we come across something similar in nature, we immediately attribute it to random processes.

It is time to admit the truth that the whole creation cries out: there is a Creator! Everything around us bears witness to His wisdom and to His love! He invites us into a living relationship with Him!

“The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork.” (Psalm 19:1)
