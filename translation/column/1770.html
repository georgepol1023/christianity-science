A tribute to brother Leonidas Fengos

It was spring 1998 when the telephone rang. An unknown voice at the other end: “The Lord told me to send you some articles.” “Gladly – what are they about?” “You’ll see,” he answered mysteriously.

A few days later a fairly large parcel came in the post. I opened it with emotion. Inside there were more than 20 articles, in English, on the well-known debate: “Evolution or Creation?”.
Puzzled, I put them in a desk drawer. My thoughts went back about ten years. I was in London for further training. On Sundays, when I had nothing else to do, I visited the museums. How dull life is without Christ, without brothers and sisters… I missed my family very much too: Stella, Christoforos, Dimitris. In those days there was not even Skype. A strong attraction drew me very often to the Natural History Museum. It was not so much the huge number of exhibits that drew me as the Darwin wing. There was a wax reconstruction of his study, and the whole wing was devoted to persuading the visitor that evolution was the only “scientific” explanation for the origin of man. The examples on which this arbitrary and unscientific claim rested could not stand up to the slightest scrutiny. I still remember my puzzlement, and the certainty inside me that a great deception was hidden here.

Ten days later brother Louis phoned me. “I want you to take on the Christianity & Science column in the newspaper.” “Brother, let me pray and I’ll tell you.” A heavy responsibility… “Lord, what am I to write? One month I’ll describe the heart, the next the kidneys and the next the lungs. I’ll soon run dry!” It was as if I heard His voice: “I have sent you material to begin with.” The parcel of articles… A deep emotion, together with awe, came over me. The Lord is with us. His grace and His mercy follow us.
In August 1998 the first article appeared in the newspaper: THE THEORY OF EVOLUTION: THE COLLAPSE OF A MYTH. To this day (19 years) the Lord has not left us. His eye was upon us… as was brother Louis’s. “Watch out for two things,” he told me again and again. “Never let the cross of Christ be missing from any article or any broadcast. Otherwise you risk falling into empty talk. And watch out: no more than 400 words in each article. Otherwise you’ll fall into chatter, and into sin.”

From 31 January 2008, now as the “Christianity & Science” team, we also began the broadcasts, “let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves” (Philippians 2:3). Our God-given longing is to proclaim everywhere that the absolute truth is the Word of God. No, we have not “borne the burden and heat of the day” (Matthew 20:12); it was the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ that gave, and gives, grace and strength.
We thank God for our brother Louis, because he did not leave us idle, like lost coins in the house of God, but used us in His work, arming us with the humble mind that so marked him.

Soon the radio station will open again. Brother Louis longed to see our country watered with the Word of God, and sprouting. We miss his physical presence, but through his example we hear him saying: “This is the way, walk ye in it” (Isaiah 30:21).
