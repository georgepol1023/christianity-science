Does the Bible teach that the Earth is flat?

In recent years the belief that the Earth is flat has found fertile ground in various Christian circles – and, what is more, that there is a vast worldwide conspiracy to hide the truth and plunge the world into darkness. The supporters of this theory even use verses from the Word of God to back up their view, and they organise international conferences and events to promote their idea.

But what does the Word of God really say? Why is there such great misinterpretation of the Scriptures? How is it possible for anyone in the year 2025 to stubbornly deny reality and turn a blind eye to obvious facts?

What the Word of God says

Several verses of Scripture say that the Earth has foundations and a base so that it should not be moved for ever. In Psalm 104:5 we read: “Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever”, and in 1 Samuel 2:8: “…for the pillars of the earth are the LORD’s, and he hath set the world upon them”.

Revelation 7:1 also tells us: “And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth…”

None of this means that the Earth has a base, rests on pillars and has four corners like a rectangle. It means that the Lord, through the laws of nature, keeps the natural balances of the Earth in perfect harmony. The Earth moves with perfect mathematical order in the universe, and there are the four points of the compass. Indeed, the time will come when God will allow all these balances to be disturbed, during the seven years and the great tribulation (see Revelation 6:12–17).

On the other hand, verses such as Job 26:7, “He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing”, and Isaiah 40:22, “It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers”, show that the Earth is suspended in space, moves under forces acting at a distance, and is spherical in shape.

Scientific proofs that the Earth is spherical

Although it is clearly not our aim to give an exhaustive list of the dozens of arguments for a spherical Earth, we will confine ourselves to the simplest and easiest to understand.

The curve of the horizon: The higher we go (for example in an aeroplane), the more visible the curve of the horizon becomes.

Journeys around the world: If the Earth were flat, no one could travel in a straight line and come back to the starting point, which is what happens on journeys by air and sea.

Photographs from space: Images from satellites and space missions show the Earth as a sphere from every angle.

The Earth’s shadow on the Moon: During a lunar eclipse the Earth casts a round shadow on the Moon, proving that it is spherical.

The different positions of the stars: Different constellations are visible from different latitudes, which would not happen if the Earth were flat.

The difference in the length of the day: At different longitudes and latitudes the length of the day changes with the season, which can only be explained if the Earth is spherical and rotates.

Satellites and satellite communications: Satellites orbiting the Earth could not stay in steady motion if the Earth were flat.

Conclusion

We need great care when we draw scientific conclusions from the Bible, because in the past many interpreters held pseudoscientific views and brought the Word of the Lord into disrepute. They claimed that Scripture teaches the geocentric model and that the Earth stands still at the centre of the universe.

But it is impossible for the Author of the Bible and the Creator of all things, who are one and the same Person, ever to contradict Himself. Whenever we read the Word, we must ask for the light of the Holy Spirit, “because they are spiritually discerned.”
