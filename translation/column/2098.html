Biology, monogamy and the human race

Marriage between one man and one woman, to create a new family that will last for life, is not a human invention. It did not develop in some ancient civilisation, nor was it devised by some wise men of antiquity to serve certain social institutions as well as possible.

It is something that the Creator of the universe Himself established and blessed in the garden of Eden. It is a blessed union that mathematics cannot describe and evolutionary biology cannot explain. For in the marriage our heavenly Father established, 1+1 makes 1 again, and the aim is not simply to continue the species but to reflect the relationship between Christ and the Church.

On 21 November 2021 a “scientific” article was published in a Greek newspaper with a large circulation, under the title: “Biology cancels monogamy”1. In it the writer tries to examine man in the light of evolutionary thinking, with the continuation of the species as the only aim. From this he concludes that monogamy and faithfulness cannot be explained by scientists, and that they go against the laws of biology and against the rest of the animal kingdom. The text contains 3 basic claims that need particular examination, as they are increasingly accepted as true by society at large, while they are in complete contradiction with the Word of God, which cannot lie.

We decide what is moral and immoral. After the first lockdown, various dating platforms recorded a dramatic rise in interest in casual relationships. Many people even searched for key words such as “ethical non-monogamy” and “polyamory”. But what does ethical non-monogamy mean?

Man is simply a part of the animal kingdom. And as part of the animal kingdom, man’s only aim is to continue the species, with multiple sexual relationships so as to sample a wide range of genetic characteristics. Monogamy leaves us “going round in circles in the shallow end of the gene pool”.

Marriage serves only certain social purposes. The father and mother stay together only to bring up their children, for the security the father’s presence provides, and for financial reasons.

How far these thoughts and claims are from what the Lord had in His heart when He created the first family!

“… so then they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.” (Mark 10:8–9)

You are not simply a higher evolved animal, moving and acting under inner drives to ensure healthy offspring. The very hand of the Creator made you, with incredible love and care. You are made in the image and likeness of God, destined to live for ever in His glory!

He has not destined you to live in a miserable, compromised marriage for the good of the children or of society. He wants your marriage to be a wonderful, loving relationship that bears witness to all people of the unimaginable future relationship of Christ and the Church.

“Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it…” (Ephesians 5:25)

And finally, there is no morality outside the bounds set by the Lawgiver. There is no joy far from the Creator. And there is no love far from our heavenly Father.

When you entrust your life to the Lord and dedicate your family to Him, you will begin to taste God’s blessings as the psalmist describes them:

“Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table. Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the LORD.” (Psalm 128:3–4)
