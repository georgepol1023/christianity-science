Why the Bible stands above all other sacred books (Part 1)

Very often, in all sorts of conversations with people, one question dominates: “Why should we accept Christianity and its sacred book, the Bible, as true, and not some other sacred book of some other great religion?”

“We happened to be born in the Western world, and that is why we accepted Christianity,” some claim. “If we had been born in Africa or in India, we would have accepted the religions there and their own sacred books as inspired and true.”

The truth, however, is that they cannot all be true, since they teach completely different and incompatible things about the nature of God, sin, forgiveness, life after death and the salvation of man. How, then, can we be sure which of all the sacred books is worthy of the honour of being called the Word of God, and what are the criteria by which we can reach that conclusion with certainty?

In these two articles we will briefly set out some basic points – tests of reliability and truth – in which the Bible far surpasses all other sacred books.

  1. The Bible is the oldest religious book, and at the same time the most modern.

The writing of the Bible began in 1500 BC with Moses and was completed at the end of the 1st century AD with the Revelation of John. However, according to many indications, Moses based his information for Genesis on earlier reliable written and oral sources, which appear in the book of Genesis about ten times as tôledôth (“generations”: historical beginnings, records). The book of Job, too, describes events that took place around 2000 BC.

At the same time it remains the most modern book, because it answers the fundamental questions of every person, as well as the social and moral problems of every age.

  1. The Bible is the bestseller of the ages.

A sacred book ought to be easy to pass on in every part of the world and in every age, and to have been translated into every language and dialect.

Unlike the sacred books of Hinduism and other religions, which are vast and out of reach for the ordinary believer and reader, the Bible consists of 66 small books and runs to about 773,000 words. Only the Koran is shorter: it is a quarter of the length of the Bible.

But the New Testament, the Christians’ book above all others, runs to only 140,000 words, made up of 27 small books – mostly letters – with one message: the worship of God and the redemption of man.

Moreover, the Bible has proved itself the bestseller not only of sacred books but of all books. It is the bestseller of the ages!

Written in three languages (Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek) on three continents by many authors, it has been and still is published in more copies than the works of Shakespeare, Marx, Darwin, Mao, the great philosophers and other bestsellers. To date it has been translated into 2,377 languages and dialects, and it is available to 90% of the world’s population. It is estimated that from the time of Gutenberg (1454) until today more than 6,000,000,000 copies have been published! Every second that passes, a new copy is printed.

  1. Only the Bible links religion with history

The events described in the Bible are not outside the framework of world history. They are fully set, in time, place and culture, within the history of the people concerned.

This is in contrast with the sacred books of other religions, which are indifferent to history and show an obvious lack of historical orientation. The well-known sacred books of the East, and the Koran, have nothing to do with history. They are more abstract philosophical and moral systems, of statements or commands, and their moral teachings can “stand” independently of their founder.

It has been calculated that chapter 10 of Genesis alone contains five times more geographical information than the whole of the Koran.

Jesus is a historical person who walked and acted in real towns and villages of a particular time. He is the one who began “both to do and teach”, walking and acting in our own real world. That is why His teaching is inseparably bound up with His person.

God willing, in the next article we will see three or four more points on which the Bible stands above all other sacred books. For “the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.” (Hebrews 4:12)
