Does atheism have answers?

Until now, in the “Christianity & Science” column, we have set out in detail what the Word of God, which cannot lie, says on fundamental questions: from the creation of the world and the appearance of life to the spiritual nature of man and his inborn urge to seek the truth. In the face of every challenge and question, we have patiently presented the scientific evidence that is in complete harmony with the revelation of the Holy Spirit through the Scriptures.

But we feel the time has come to examine the strong scientific evidence that leads well-known scientists and ordinary people to make statements about “proof that God does not exist…”. In his Letter to a Christian Nation, the American philosopher Sam Harris claims that atheism is not a philosophy or a worldview, but simply the admission of the obvious. And the American physicist and cosmologist Lawrence Krauss, a fervent atheist, claims that his scientific discoveries deny the existence of God.1

What, then, are these strong arguments of theirs? Are they really unshakeable scientific proofs? Must a Christian pupil or student reject scientific knowledge if he wants to follow God? Does faith in a Creator God, who is the only source of life, contradict what science investigates and discovers?

The British professor of chemistry Peter Atkins claims that “the whole universe is an elaborate and engaging rearrangement of nothing”, while the British biologist Richard Dawkins tells us that “the blind forces of physics could build these complex machines” – that is, human beings.

Do these statements stand up to closer examination? Because they come from the lips of famous scientists, are they therefore scientific statements?

The Lord Himself, 2,700 years ago, sets the same challenge to all who deny His existence with arguments: “…bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob. Let them bring them forth, and shew us what shall happen: let them shew the former things, what they be, that we may consider them, and know the latter end of them” (Isaiah 41:21–22)

How can I claim that the universe emerged from nothing without breaking the principle of causality? How can I explain the origin of life, since every living cell comes from another? How are compassion, philotimo and the reproach of conscience to be explained if man is the evolutionary product of blind processes? And finally, how can I regard death as the end of existence if Jesus rose again? We will deal with these questions in our next articles.

The Word of God has never been afraid of honest, critical thinking. For it is the only one that can answer all of man’s fundamental questions. “Every word of God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their trust in him”, the writer of Proverbs tells us (Proverbs 30:5).

And beyond knowledge, it can give meaning, purpose and hope to every soul that seeks it. For Christ did not come only to give answers… He came to give you eternal salvation! In our days, more relevant than ever, the voice of the Lord can be heard again, calling out to all of us: “And let him that is athirst come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.” (Revelation 22:17)
