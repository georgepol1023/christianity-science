“Of one blood…”

These words are part of the sermon that the apostle Paul preached, by the Holy Spirit, on the Areopagus in Athens about 2,000 years ago. Anyone can read the whole sermon in 5 minutes, in the 17th chapter of the Acts of the Apostles, verses 12–31.

He spoke to them about Jesus and the Resurrection. He brought them the good news of God’s eternal love, and with just half a verse he demolished the strongholds of empty philosophy. God, he told them, made the world – the world did not make God, as the Epicurean philosophers believed and materialists still believe today. And secondly, everything is not God in a pantheistic unity, as the Stoic philosophers believed then and, above all, the followers of Eastern religions still believe today.

Among other things, the apostle of the Gentiles told them that God “hath made of one blood all nations of men…” (Acts 17:26). This left his hearers literally speechless. How could the apostle Paul say this? Had he perhaps studied molecular biology or genetic engineering? Of course not; but he believed absolutely in the written Word of God, which tells us that all people come from Adam, whom God formed from the dust of the ground (Genesis 2:7), and from Eve, whom God made from Adam’s side (Genesis 2:22). The Word of God did not let him down!

“I have more understanding than all my teachers: for thy testimonies are my meditation. I understand more than the ancients, because I keep thy precepts.” (Psalm 119:99–100).

This truth has been disputed and fought against, and still is, by many. But modern science comes to confirm, in the most categorical way, that the Bible is right. One simple example: if a white person needs a heart or kidney transplant, the right organ can come from a black person, or the other way round! Indeed, the study of human genetic material has shown that between any two people the differences are no more than 0.2%. That is, the difference between two white people, or two black people, or between a white and a black person, is the same, and no more than 0.2%. And the differences in outward appearance are reckoned at 6% of that 0.2% – that is, no more than 0.012%!

“of one blood…” As these lines are written, rivers of blood are being shed – not only on battlefields but in cities and neighbourhoods, in workplaces and even in homes – by people who share the same blood. If this sentence had come from the mouth of a man, it would hold astonishment, endless grief and despair. But it was spoken by the Holy Spirit, and God has a wonderful plan to restore fallen man and let him live with Him for ever.

THROUGH THE BLOOD OF JESUS CHRIST…

A plan that God had before the foundation of the world! “And if ye call on the Father, who without respect of persons judgeth according to every man’s work, pass the time of your sojourning here in fear: forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers; but with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot: who verily was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you, who by him do believe in God, that raised him up from the dead, and gave him glory; that your faith and hope might be in God.” (1 Peter 1:17–21)

“…as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned” (Romans 5:12)

There had to be redemption, the forgiveness of sins, atonement (satisfying the just and holy nature of God), and much else that we see only dimly, as through a glass. The Triune God, the only true God, accomplished the great work of man’s salvation. At its centre is the offering of the blood of Christ!

“…in whom we have redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins” (Colossians 1:14),

“…in whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace” (Ephesians 1:7)

It is a fact! God “…hath made of one blood all nations of men…” and now He offers eternal life freely through the blood of His only begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ.

Blessed is the person who will sincerely repent and gratefully accept the great gift of eternal life THROUGH THE PRECIOUS BLOOD OF JESUS CHRIST!

The offer is free and for everyone, but a person must take it while he is alive. After that comes the judgement. “And the times of this ignorance God winked at; but now commandeth all men every where to repent: because he hath appointed a day, in the which he will judge the world in righteousness by that man whom he hath ordained; whereof he hath given assurance unto all men, in that he hath raised him from the dead.” (Acts 17:30–31)
